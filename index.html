The difference

An adviser who arranges finance — not a broker chasing commission.

“I'm a chartered accountant. I read your numbers properly and tell you the truth — including when the answer is don't borrow.”

Most commercial finance brokers are salespeople who learned finance. We're the other way round: a chartered management accountant and finance director who happens to arrange funding.

That matters when you're borrowing to buy a business, fund a buyout or take over from a retiring owner — deals that need structuring, not just a lender who says yes. We look at whether the numbers work, what you can service, and how to shape the facility so it completes and doesn't come back to bite you.

We're paid a commission by the lender, and we'll always tell you what it is, in writing, before you commit. No surprises — that's rather the point.
